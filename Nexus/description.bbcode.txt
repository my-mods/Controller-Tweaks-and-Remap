[size=5][b][color=#E8C88A]Controller Tweaks and Remap[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />Remap your controller for The Blood of Dawnwalker. Choose your combat and exploration buttons, walk more comfortably, and open the Map or Game Hub with a short or long press of one button.
<br />
<br />[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]What the mod does[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />[list]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Quickslot holds:[/color][/b] D-pad quickslot items are used on short release. Long holds do not consume them, including torch holds with Ignite.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Shoulder and trigger layout:[/color][/b] in the Default preset, attack and the overworld ability wheel use RT / R2, block uses LT / L2, Focus and combat abilities use LB / L1, and Shadowstep uses RB / R1. Photo Mode uses RB / R1 to rise and LB / L1 to fall.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Easier walking:[/color][/b] a wider part of the stick's travel keeps you walking. You can still sprint, and Shapeshift and Mercurial Fervour stay active when you push the stick fully.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Map and Game Hub on one button:[/color][/b] short press View / Touchpad for the Map; long press for the Game Hub. The long press takes 0.30 seconds.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Controller stutter fixes:[/color][/b] fixes controller-related stutter and restores your chosen controls after loading a save.[/*]
<br />[/list]The [b]Alternative preset[/b] uses X / Square for attack, Y / Triangle for block, LT / L2 for Focus, RT / R2 for the overworld ability wheel, and LB / L1 for Bite. You can customize either preset through Mod Settings.
<br />
<br />[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]What you can remap[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />Mod Settings gives you [b]31 controller settings[/b]:
<br />
<br />[list]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Combat and abilities:[/color][/b] attack, block, dodge, weapons, target lock and switching, Focus, Bite, Shadowstep, and ability controls. Attack and the overworld ability wheel can use different buttons.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Exploration and menus:[/color][/b] interaction, jump, crouch, sprint, Planeshift, quickslots, the controls legend, Map and Game Hub.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Sticks and Photo Mode:[/color][/b] choose the left or right stick for movement and camera look, and change Photo Mode's rise, fall and exit buttons.[/*]
<br />[/list]Button choices include face buttons, shoulders, triggers, stick clicks, the D-pad, View / Touchpad, Menu / Options, and right-stick left or right. Movement and camera look must use a stick.
<br />
<br />[b]Inherit preset[/b] uses the Default or Alternative layout you selected in the game, including this mod's preset bindings. Choose a specific button to use your own assignment instead.
<br />
<br />[size=3][b][color=#A8D5C2]Choosing your shoulder buttons[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />For the game's original Default shoulder layout, select block on LB / L1; Focus and combat abilities on LT / L2; attack and the overworld ability wheel on RB / R1; Shadowstep on RT / R2. Set Photo Mode rise to RT / R2 and fall to LT / L2.
<br />
<br />[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Controls that stay linked[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />These actions share a binding. Assigning a button moves them together; you cannot give each action its own button:
<br />
<br />[list]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Attack:[/color][/b] attack, Death from Above, and drawing your weapon with the attack button.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Block:[/color][/b] block and drawing your weapon with the block button.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Dodge:[/color][/b] dodge and sprinting during combat.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Interact:[/color][/b] ordinary interaction and ledge-look interaction.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Jump:[/color][/b] jump and ladder drop.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Crouch:[/color][/b] crouch, ladder slide and ability cancel.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Drink Blood:[/color][/b] starting Voracious Bite in Focus, holding to feed, and Necrospeak in Focus.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Movement and camera sticks:[/color][/b] each also controls its Photo Mode counterpart.[/*]
<br />[/list][size=3][b][color=#A8D5C2]Using the same button for several actions[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />[list]
<br />[*]Keep Bite on a different button from both Attack and the button you hold for Focus. Keep the ability wheel off that Focus button too.[/*]
<br />[*]Focus actions take priority over ordinary interaction while Focus is active. Alternative can use X / Square for both Attack and Interact. Outside Focus, Interact works normally.[/*]
<br />[*]Two actions within Focus can still trigger together if you give them the same button. The mod does not resolve every possible button conflict.[/*]
<br />[*]Keep Map and Game Hub on the same button for their short press / long press shortcut.[/*]
<br />[/list][size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Limits[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />[list]
<br />[*]Remapping covers the listed controller controls. It does not add button combinations, macros, or custom press/hold behaviours.[/*]
<br />[*]The walking range and Map/Game Hub hold time are fixed. There are no settings to adjust them or turn them off individually.[/*]
<br />[*][b]Runtime remapping[/b] is not a switch for the whole mod. When it is Off, the Default shoulder swap, walking tweaks and Map/Game Hub shortcut stay active.[/*]
<br />[*]On-screen button prompts and the game's controller diagram may still show the original layout.[/*]
<br />[*]This menu remaps controller input. Use the game's own settings to change keyboard bindings.[/*]

<br />[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Reporting Bugs and other issues[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />If you encounter an issue, set [b]Logging[/b] to [b]Debug[/b] in this mod’s Mod Setting Menu settings, apply the change, reproduce the issue. Then start either a bug report in the Bugs tab on Nexus (preferred method for me to track things), or at least start a new thread in comments and send me [b]Dawnwalker/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/UE4SS.log[/b] from your game folder. Do not report unrelated bugs in other people's threads, please, this makes it impossible for me to track issues.
<br />
<br />
<br />[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]How to change your controls[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />Mod Setting Menu is required. Apply saves changes and updates gameplay. Enable HookProcessConsoleExec = 1 in your Vortex-managed UE4SS configuration.
<br />
<br />Settings are prepared when the game starts.
<br />
<br />Open [b]Mod Settings &gt; All Mods &gt; Controller Tweaks and Remap[/b]. Choose your controls, press [b]Apply[/b] to use the changes during play.
<br />
<br />[b]Restore[/b] discards changes you have not applied. [b]Reset[/b] selects this mod's defaults. Choose Off, Error, Warning, Info or Debug. Warning is the default; Off is silent. Debug records detailed troubleshooting information in Dawnwalker/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/UE4SS.log.
