[size=5][b][color=#E8C88A]Controller Tweaks and Remap[/color][/b][/size]

Remap your controller for The Blood of Dawnwalker. Choose your combat and exploration buttons, walk more comfortably, and open the Map or Game Hub with a short or long press of one button.

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]What the mod does[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Shoulder and trigger layout:[/color][/b] in the Default preset, attack and the overworld ability wheel use RT / R2, block uses LT / L2, Focus and combat abilities use LB / L1, and Shadowstep uses RB / R1. Photo Mode uses RB / R1 to rise and LB / L1 to fall.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Easier walking:[/color][/b] a wider part of the stick's travel keeps you walking. You can still sprint, and Shapeshift and Mercurial Fervour stay active when you push the stick fully.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Map and Game Hub on one button:[/color][/b] short press View / Touchpad for the Map; long press for the Game Hub. The long press takes 0.30 seconds.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Controller stutter fixes:[/color][/b] fixes controller-related stutter and restores your chosen controls after loading a save.
[/list]

The [b]Alternative preset[/b] uses X / Square for attack, Y / Triangle for block, LT / L2 for Focus, RT / R2 for the overworld ability wheel, and LB / L1 for Bite. You can customize either preset through Mod Settings.

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]What you can remap[/color][/b][/size]

Mod Settings gives you [b]31 controller settings[/b]:

[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Combat and abilities:[/color][/b] attack, block, dodge, weapons, target lock and switching, Focus, Bite, Shadowstep, and ability controls. Attack and the overworld ability wheel can use different buttons.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Exploration and menus:[/color][/b] interaction, jump, crouch, sprint, Planeshift, quickslots, the controls legend, Map and Game Hub.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Sticks and Photo Mode:[/color][/b] choose the left or right stick for movement and camera look, and change Photo Mode's rise, fall and exit buttons.
[/list]

Button choices include face buttons, shoulders, triggers, stick clicks, the D-pad, View / Touchpad, Menu / Options, and right-stick left or right. Movement and camera look must use a stick.

[b]Inherit preset[/b] uses the Default or Alternative layout you selected in the game, including this mod's preset bindings. Choose a specific button to use your own assignment instead.

[size=3][b][color=#A8D5C2]Choosing your shoulder buttons[/color][/b][/size]

For the game's original Default shoulder layout, select block on LB / L1; Focus and combat abilities on LT / L2; attack and the overworld ability wheel on RB / R1; Shadowstep on RT / R2. Set Photo Mode rise to RT / R2 and fall to LT / L2.

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Controls that stay linked[/color][/b][/size]

These actions share a binding. Assigning a button moves them together; you cannot give each action its own button:

[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Attack:[/color][/b] attack, Death from Above, and drawing your weapon with the attack button.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Block:[/color][/b] block and drawing your weapon with the block button.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Dodge:[/color][/b] dodge and sprinting during combat.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Interact:[/color][/b] ordinary interaction and ledge-look interaction.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Jump:[/color][/b] jump and ladder drop.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Crouch:[/color][/b] crouch, ladder slide and ability cancel.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Drink Blood:[/color][/b] starting Voracious Bite in Focus, holding to feed, and Necrospeak in Focus.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Movement and camera sticks:[/color][/b] each also controls its Photo Mode counterpart.
[/list]

[size=3][b][color=#A8D5C2]Using the same button for several actions[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Keep Bite on a different button from both Attack and the button you hold for Focus. Keep the ability wheel off that Focus button too.
[*]Focus actions take priority over ordinary interaction while Focus is active. Alternative can use X / Square for both Attack and Interact. Outside Focus, Interact works normally.
[*]Two actions within Focus can still trigger together if you give them the same button. The mod does not resolve every possible button conflict.
[*]Keep Map and Game Hub on the same button for their short press / long press shortcut.
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Limits[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Remapping covers the listed controller controls. It does not add button combinations, macros, or custom press/hold behaviours.
[*]The walking range and Map/Game Hub hold time are fixed. There are no settings to adjust them or turn them off individually.
[*][b]Runtime remapping[/b] is not a switch for the whole mod. When it is Off, the Default shoulder swap, walking tweaks and Map/Game Hub shortcut stay active.
[*]On-screen button prompts and the game's controller diagram may still show the original layout.
[*]This menu remaps controller input. Use the game's own settings to change keyboard bindings.
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Requirements[/color][/b][/size]

Install [b]one UE4SS option[/b] and [b]Mod Setting Menu[/b]:

[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]UE4SS — choose one:[/color][/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/283]Framecore's UE4SS for BoD (2b or later)[/url] OR [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/18]Vercadi's UE4SS for Dawnwalker (RC6 or later)[/url].
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Also required:[/color][/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/271]Mod Setting Menu (1.0.5.1 or later)[/url].

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Installation[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Vortex:[/color][/b] Install Controller-Tweaks-and-Remap.zip through Vortex, enable it and deploy.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Manual:[/color][/b] Copy the archive's Dawnwalker folder into ...\steamapps\common\The Blood of Dawnwalker\, preserving the folder structure.
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]How to change your controls[/color][/b][/size]

On first use, [b]load a save once[/b], then return to the main menu.

Open [b]Mod Settings > All Mods > Controller Tweaks and Remap[/b]. Choose your controls, press [b]Apply[/b], then [b]load a save[/b] to use the changes.

[b]Restore[/b] discards changes you have not applied. [b]Reset[/b] selects this mod's defaults. Leave [b]Logging[/b] Off for normal play; turn it On only for troubleshooting.
