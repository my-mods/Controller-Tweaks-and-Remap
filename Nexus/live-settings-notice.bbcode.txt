[size=5][b][color=#E8C88A]Author's Notes[/color][/b][/size]

Fully configurable input map for the controller. Use at your own risk for very peculiar configs, like multiple bindings on the same key.

Use [b][color=#A8D5C2]Mod Settings[/color][/b] to change your bindings. See the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/203?tab=description]mod's description[/url] for the current controls, configuration details, and advice on using the same button for several actions.

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]1.5.2 Stutters[/color][/b][/size]
Stutter troubles are resolved as of 1.5.2.

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]1.5.3 Experimental fixes for non-conventional layouts[/color][/b][/size]
Experimental fixes improve button remapping so related actions follow your chosen controls, address shared-button conflicts affecting Death from Above in Focus mode, and let you assign Attack and the ability wheel to separate buttons.

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]New: change settings while playing[/color][/b][/size]

The latest update lets you change this mod’s settings [b]without reloading your save[/b]. Open [b][color=#A8D5C2]Mod Settings[/color][/b], make your changes and press [b][color=#A8D5C2]Apply[/color][/b] to use them straight away.

[b][color=#A8D5C2]You need:[/color][/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/271]Mod Setting Menu[/url] [b]1.0.6 or newer[/b].

For live Apply, keep [code]HookProcessConsoleExec = 1[/code] enabled in your Vortex-managed UE4SS configuration.
